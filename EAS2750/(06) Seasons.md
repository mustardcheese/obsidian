Amount of solar radiation varies where you on are earth:

**Shortwave Radiation**, what makes it hot outside
- Earth's rotation
- Earth's orbit around the sun
- Solar angle
- Cloud coverage
- Albedo (reflection)

Around the equator, the radiation is completely perpendicular to the earth, meaning it passes through less atmosphere before affecting the surface.

Around the poles, the radiation is more angled, meaning is passes through more atmosphere before affecting the surface.

### Solar Angle
$S = S_0\ sin(q)$
$S_0 = 1361 Wm^{-2}$
$q = (90-lattitude)+solar\ declanation$
