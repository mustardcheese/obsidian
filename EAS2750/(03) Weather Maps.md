### Isopleths on weather maps
Isobars -- lines of constant pressure (surface maps)
Isotherms -- lines of constant temperature
Isotachs -- lines of constant wind speed
Isohypses -- lines of constant geopotential height (upper tropospheric maps)

### How to interpret Sea Level Pressure Value
1. If value < 500 -> place 10 in front of coded value. If value > 500 -> place 9 in front of value
	1. If value = 500, depends on surrounding pressure valuess
2. Place decimal at last digit
3. Final value is pressure in "mb" rounded to nearest tenth

229 -> 10229 -> 1022.9mb

Height vs Geopotential Height
- Height is the distance above the surface
- Geopotential Height is the distance above the surface assuming gravity does not vary with latitude and altitude
	- Gravity varies around the earth, Geopotential accounts for this
	- Geopotential: Amount of work performed against gravity ot raise 1kg mass to some height above surface
