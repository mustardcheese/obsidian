## Matrix Algorithms
- Regular structure
- Well-suited to static partitioning of computation
- Computation is partitioned based on the data
	- Input, Output, Intermediate

## Matrix-Vector Multiplication

Problem: Multiply a dense $n\times n$ matrix $A$ with a $n \times 1$ vector $x$
Yield the $n \times 1$ result vector y
Serial Algorithm requires O($n^2$) multiplications and additions
